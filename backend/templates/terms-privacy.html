5Tokens & Subscriptions

Enejistats uses a token economy to facilitate certain platform actions. Token costs and subscription prices are set exclusively by platform administrators and are not fixed. Current prices are always available at enejistats.com/pricing.html and via the platform API.

Token acquisition: Tokens may be purchased directly or earned through Data Scout activities.

Subscriptions: Subscription plans provide enhanced features and may include token allocations. Subscription prices, periods, and features are admin-controlled.

Refunds: Token purchases are non-refundable once tokens have been used. Unused tokens are non-transferable and forfeited upon account termination. Subscription fees are non-refundable once a billing period has commenced.

7Liveness Verification & Data Retention

Certain registration and verification processes on Enejistats require a liveness check — a short analysis performed through your device's camera in real time. This requirement applies to Player, Club, Agent, Scout, DCO, and Supervisor registrations.

Purpose: Liveness verification is used solely to confirm the physical presence of the user at the time of registration, and to prevent fraudulent account creation.

Data Retention: No video is recorded, stored, or transmitted. The liveness check is performed entirely on-device using AI face analysis. The metadata record (timestamp, user ID, purpose) is retained for audit purposes.

Consent: By proceeding through a liveness check, you explicitly consent to the temporary processing of your biometric data for identity verification purposes on your device only.
